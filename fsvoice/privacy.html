Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FsVoice.Demo handles information when you use the app.

Overview

FsVoice.Demo is a voice-enabled demo app that may use your device's microphone and the OpenAI API to provide realtime voice interaction and AI-generated responses.

We do not sell your personal information, use microphone audio for advertising, or use microphone audio for cross-app tracking.

Information the App May Process

FsVoice.Demo may process the following information when you use app features:

  • Microphone audio: audio captured when you choose to use a voice feature.
  • Transcripts or text input: text produced from your speech or text you enter into the app.
  • AI responses: responses generated by the OpenAI API for the voice or chat session.
  • App settings: local preferences or configuration needed for the app to function.
  • Technical data: limited technical information needed to make API requests, diagnose errors, or operate the app.

Microphone Access

FsVoice.Demo may request access to your device's microphone to provide voice input, realtime audio processing, and AI voice-response features.

Microphone access is used only when you choose to use a feature that requires audio input. You can disable microphone access at any time in your device settings. Some voice features may not work if microphone access is disabled.

OpenAI API Processing

When you use a voice or AI feature, microphone audio, transcripts, text prompts, conversation context, and related technical data may be transmitted to OpenAI, our artificial intelligence service provider, to process your request and generate realtime responses.

Audio may be converted into text or other intermediate representations as part of providing the requested functionality. OpenAI may process API inputs and outputs according to OpenAI's API data policies, retention settings, and applicable service terms.

OpenAI states that API data is not used to train or improve OpenAI models by default unless the API account holder explicitly opts in. OpenAI may retain API data for abuse monitoring, security, legal, or operational purposes according to its applicable policies and account settings.

Do Not Share Sensitive Information

Because voice input and prompts may be sent to OpenAI for processing, you should avoid sharing sensitive personal information, confidential information, passwords, financial account information, health information, or other information that you do not want processed by an external AI service.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

FsVoice.Demo does not use third-party advertising or behavioral tracking.

FsVoice.Demo does not sell microphone audio, transcripts, prompts, or AI responses to advertisers or data brokers.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app may store settings or app-related data locally on your device to provide its functionality. This local data remains on your device unless you delete the app or otherwise remove the data through device or app settings.

Third-Party Services

FsVoice.Demo uses OpenAI as a third-party service provider for AI audio, text, and realtime response processing. Your use of features powered by OpenAI may be subject to OpenAI's own policies and terms.

If you download the app from the Apple App Store or use Apple platform features, Apple may process certain information according to Apple's own privacy policies and App Store terms. We do not control Apple's collection or processing of information.

See Third-Party Software Notices for open-source dependency and service-provider notices.

Children's Privacy

FsVoice.Demo is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can address the issue.

Data Sharing

We share information with OpenAI only as needed to provide AI voice, audio, transcription, or response functionality requested through the app.

We may also disclose information if required to comply with applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
